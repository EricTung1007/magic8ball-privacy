Privacy Policy for Magic 8 Ball

Last Updated: October 27, 2026

1. Introduction

Magic 8 Ball (“the App”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

The App provides an interactive Magic 8 Ball experience, personalized features, advertisements, and optional in-app purchases.

2. Information Stored Locally

The App may store the following information locally on your device:

* Questions and interaction history
* Personalization preferences
* App settings
* Premium feature preferences

This information is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to servers operated by the App developer.

3. Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Google AdMob and its advertising partners may automatically collect certain information, including:

* Device identifiers
* Advertising identifiers, where permitted
* Advertising interaction data
* Device and application information
* Approximate location derived from network information

This information may be used for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention, and related purposes.

For more information, please refer to Google’s Privacy Policy:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. In-App Purchases

The App offers optional in-app purchases, including premium features and advertisement removal.

Purchases are processed through Apple’s App Store payment system.

The App developer does not directly collect or store your credit card or payment details.

Apple may process transaction information according to its own privacy policies.

5. Data Sharing

The App developer does not sell personal information collected directly through the App.

Information may be processed by third-party services, including Google AdMob and Apple, as described in this policy.

6. Data Security

User-generated content and personalization preferences are stored locally on your device.

The App developer does not operate a remote database for storing these records.

Third-party services maintain their own security practices.

7. Children’s Privacy

The App is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13.

Third-party advertising services may process information according to their applicable policies and legal requirements.

8. Your Choices

You may manage or remove locally stored information using the App’s available settings, where supported.

You may also uninstall the App to remove its locally stored data, subject to your device’s backup and data retention behavior.

Advertising privacy preferences can be managed through your device’s privacy settings, where available.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes in the App, applicable laws, or third-party services.

Updates will be published on this page with a revised date.
